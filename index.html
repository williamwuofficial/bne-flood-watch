About BNE Flood Watch

An unofficial, open-source map of storm and flood information for Greater Brisbane, from Chermside to Springwood, built only from freely available data.

Data sources & licences

  • Rain radar: RainViewer (low resolution, so cells look blocky when zoomed in)
  • Forecast: Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0)
  • Creek gauges, rainfall totals and Feb 2022 flood extent: Brisbane City Council open data (CC BY 4.0)
  • 1893, 1974 and 2011 flood extents: © State of Queensland, Queensland Spatial (FloodCheck)
  • Road events: State of Queensland (Department of Transport and Main Roads), QLDTraffic API (CC BY 4.0 AU)
  • Base map: OpenFreeMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors
